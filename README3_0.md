## Overview

NdaY'Fako is an evolving Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) initiative designed to support sustainable waste management and circular economy services at the Commune level.

Originally developed as a **multi-operator Waste Management Platform**, the project has progressively evolved into the technological foundation of a **Communal Digital Public Infrastructure (C-DPI)**, with interoperability towards Madagascar's future **National Digital Public Infrastructure (NDPI)**.

The current implementation remains fully operational as an operator-centric platform while the architecture is being extended to place the **Commune** at the centre of governance, coordination and public service delivery.

This evolutionary approach ensures continuous delivery of operational capabilities while progressively introducing the components required for a complete Digital Public Infrastructure.

The current application is fully operational as a **multi-operator waste management platform**.

- Building and Household Assessment
- QR-enabled Waste Containers
- Customer Registration
- Multi-Operator Management

- Collection Route Planning
- Pickup Tracking
- Subscription Management
- Dashboards and Reporting

---

# A Multi-Operator Ecosystem

One of the fundamental design principles of NdaY'Fako is that waste management should remain open to multiple service providers.

The platform does **not** replace operators.

Instead, it enables Communes to regulate, coordinate and supervise multiple authorised actors operating through a common Digital Public Infrastructure.

Potential operators include:

- Private Companies
- Municipal Services
- Cooperatives
- Community-Based Organisations
- NGOs
- Women's Associations
- Youth Associations
- Social Enterprises
- Recycling Enterprises

This creates a competitive, transparent and sustainable service ecosystem while ensuring public oversight.

---

# The Commune as the Operational Root

The architectural evolution introduces the Commune as the primary governance entity.

The Commune becomes responsible for:

- Waste Management Planning
- Operator Licensing
- Service Monitoring
- Environmental Compliance
- Community Awareness
- Circular Economy Promotion
- Infrastructure Management
- Territorial Performance Monitoring

Waste operators continue delivering services while operating within the communal governance framework.

---

# Towards a National Digital Public Infrastructure

NdaY'Fako is designed to become one of the sectoral Digital Public Infrastructures contributing to Madagascar's future NDPI ecosystem.

Future interoperability includes:

- National Digital Identity
- Municipal Registry
- Territorial Information Systems
- National Statistics
- Environmental Information Systems
- Digital Payment Platforms
- Notification Services
- Open Government APIs

Each Commune remains operationally autonomous while participating in a federated national ecosystem.
